Hello, I am Garrett.

I started using [Obsidian](https://obsidian.md/) around ~2020 to collect notes and organize tasks at work. I have since collected ~800 very detailed notes about every process, person, project, and tool I deal with at work. I refer back to these notes regularly, and Obsidian makes it easy to do so. Eventually, I realized if it works in the office it might work at home too.

I think its important to write ones thoughts down. And I think one is obligated to share their "art". So welcome to getdisowned.com, a collection of personal notes that are just barely coherent enough to put on the internet.
